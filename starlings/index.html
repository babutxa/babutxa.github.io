Virtual bird

A "zig" is a quick roll to one side (0.25 s) and back (0.30 s). Each bird copies a neighbour within 2 m after a 0.05 s cue time plus its reaction time, so the roll spreads as a wave. Timings from Hemelrijk, van Zuidam & Hildenbrandt (2015). Longer reaction times give a slower wave.

Drone limits

With limits off, the falcon makes the flock scatter like real starlings. That breaks the drones' acceleration budget and safe spacing, and the telemetry turns red.
